How do you ask a dating app match to get on a call?

Keep it short, casual and optional: "Want to hop on a quick call this week?" Offer a time, and be totally fine if they say no.

Pick the right moment

Ask once you've traded a few good messages and the chat feels easy. Waiting too long makes the ask feel like a bigger deal.

Words that work

  • "I'm better on the phone than over text. Want to do a quick call?"
  • "Texting is slow. Want to chat for a few minutes tonight?"
  • "Free Thursday evening for a short call?"

Make it small and easy

Say how long it will be. Ten minutes feels easy, and an open-ended call feels like a lot.

Suggest a time so they can just say yes. If a call feels too direct, ask them to call you instead.

If they say no

Take it kindly and move on. Some people need more time, and some just don't like calls.
