What do you say when someone asks "tell me about yourself" on a date?

Pick three small, specific things about your life and say them in about 30 seconds. Then ask them something back.

Why this question feels so hard

It's huge and vague, so your brain goes blank. Nobody wants to sound like a résumé or a brag.

The fix is to stop trying to describe your whole self. Just give them a few things to grab onto.

A simple three-part answer

  1. What you do day to day, in one short line.
  2. Something you love doing outside of work.
  3. One small, current detail, like a show, a recipe or a trip you're planning.

Example: "I work in marketing, I'm training for a 10K, and I'm slowly learning to make good ramen."

Make it easy for them to respond

Specific details give people something to ask about. "I like travel" gets a nod, while "I'm planning a road trip" gets a question.

End with "How about you?" so it feels like a swap, not a speech.

Things to skip

  • Your whole dating history.
  • A list of what you don't want.
  • Anything you'd say differently to a friend.

If nerves are the issue, try how to stop being nervous before a first call. For more things to ask, see first call tips.

Where it helps on Beet

On a 3-minute voice call, there's no profile to lean on, so a short, warm answer goes a long way. The call ends at 3 minutes, so a 30-second intro leaves plenty of room to talk.

FAQ

How long should my answer be?
About 30 seconds. Then ask them a question so it turns into a conversation.
Should I mention my job?
You can, but keep it to one line. Say what you enjoy about it, not just the title.
What if I freeze up?
Say "Give me a second, that's a big question," then pick one thing you did this week. Small and true beats polished.
