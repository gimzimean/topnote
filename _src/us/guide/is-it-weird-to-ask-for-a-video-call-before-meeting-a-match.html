Is it weird to ask for a video call before meeting a match?

No, it isn't weird. Keep it short and friendly, and treat it as a way to feel comfortable, not a test.

Why it's a fair ask

A short call tells you more than days of texting. You hear their voice, their humor and whether they match what you've been told.

It also helps you feel safer. Read our guide on avoiding catfishing for more.

How to ask kindly

Keep it light and specific. Offer a time and a short length, like 10 minutes.

Say why: you like to talk first, and it's the same for everyone you meet.

If they say no

Don't push or guilt them. Some people dislike video but are fine with a voice call.

If every kind of live chat gets a new excuse, that's worth noticing. See is it a red flag if a match won't get on a call.

An easier option: voice only

A voice call skips the pressure of how you look on camera. In TopNote, each 1:1 call is 3 minutes with no photos, and photos open afterward only if you both say yes.

FAQ

How do I ask without sounding suspicious?
Say it's about comfort, not doubt. Try, "I like to hop on a quick call before meeting anyone. Free this week?"
What if they say no?
One no isn't a verdict. Offer a voice call instead, and notice whether they keep dodging every kind of live chat.
Does TopNote use video?
No. TopNote calls are voice only for 3 minutes, and photos open afterward only if you both say yes.
