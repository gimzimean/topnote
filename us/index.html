Before you meet, 3 minutes first.

Ever lost an evening to someone who only looked good in photos? TopNote flips the order: talk for 3 minutes first, see photos only if it clicks.

How it works

  1. Talk for 3 minutes. No photos, no numbers. Just a voice call in the app.
  2. 3 more minutes. If you both tap it, you keep talking. Nobody knows who tapped.
  3. Photos. They show up only if you both say yes.
  4. Swap numbers. After photos, if you both want to. That's a match.

If only one of you says yes, the other person never finds out.

Not in the mood for 1:1? Join a 10-minute voice chat with 3 other members on one topic. Leave anytime.

FAQ

What is TopNote?
TopNote is a voice-first dating app. You talk with one person for 3 minutes before you see photos. If you both want to, photos open, then numbers.
When do I see their photos?
Only after the call, and only if you both say yes. If one of you says no, the other person isn't told.
Are calls recorded?
No. Calls are never recorded. You can hang up, report, or block anytime.
Who can join?
Adults 18+ with a US mobile number. Every member adds 3 photos, does a quick face check, and is reviewed by our team before their first call.
What is Table for 4?
A 10-minute voice chat with 3 other members on one topic. Afterward you can quietly pick one person. If you pick each other, you get a 3-minute 1:1 call.
When are calls?
Every evening from 5 to 11 PM Eastern Time. You pick the hour.
How much does it cost?
The app is free. You get 1 free 1:1 call every day, and your first Table for 4 is free. Call passes are sold in the app.
Can I verify my job or income?
Yes, if you want. You can verify your ID, work email, and things like income or net worth. They only show after a call, as a badge, never as exact numbers.
