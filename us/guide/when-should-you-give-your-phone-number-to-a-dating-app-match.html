When should you give your phone number to a dating app match?

T.Beet EditorsNov 17, 2026 · 2 min read

30-SECOND SUMMARY

Wait until you feel comfortable, and you don't owe anyone your number on a schedule. A good rule of thumb is to share it only after you've talked voice to voice and you both want to keep going.

01Why there's no rush

Your number is tied to your real life. Once someone has it, you can't take it back.

Staying in the app gives you room to see how a person treats your pace. That tells you a lot.

02Signs it might be time

  • You've had a real conversation, ideally by voice.
  • They've respected your boundaries so far.
  • Their story stays consistent from one chat to the next.
  • You're excited, not pressured.

03Signs to wait

They ask in the first few messages, or they get annoyed when you say not yet. Moving you off the app fast can also be a warning sign, so read our guide on spotting a romance scam.

04What to say if you're not ready

Keep it short and kind: "I'd rather keep chatting here for now." If you'd like to hear their voice first, here's how to ask for a call.

05How Beet handles it

On Beet you talk for 3 minutes, voice only, with no photos. If you both say yes, photos open, and phone numbers are swapped only if you both want to. There's no in-app chat.

Q&AFAQ

Is it a red flag if a match asks for my number right away?

Not always, but pace matters. If you say you'd like to wait and they respect that, it's fine. If they push, take that as your answer.

Can I talk to a match without giving out my number?

Yes. Keep chatting in the app, or use an app that lets you talk by voice first. On Beet, numbers are swapped only if both of you want to after seeing photos.

What can I say if I'm not ready to share it?

Try: "I'd rather keep chatting here for now. Let's see how the next few days go." A kind person will be fine with that.
