Why is texting on dating apps so exhausting?

Texting is tiring because it asks for steady effort and gives you little in return. A short voice call tells you more than days of messages, and it takes far less out of you.

Why the back-and-forth drains you

You're writing, waiting, and reading into every reply. Tone gets lost, so you end up guessing.

It also never ends. There's always one more message to answer.

Set a simple limit

Decide how much chatting you're happy with before you suggest something else. A few messages is plenty.

Then give the app a window, like 20 minutes in the evening. Close it when the time's up.

Suggest a voice call early

Try something light: "Want to hop on a quick call this week?" Hearing someone laugh tells you more than a paragraph of text.

If you're new to it, our first call tips and phone call before a first date guides can help.

FAQ

How many messages should I send before suggesting a call?
There's no magic number. A handful of back-and-forth messages is plenty, so you can suggest a call once the chat feels friendly.
What if they only want to text?
That's their choice, and you can choose too. If you want a voice, say so kindly and see how they respond.
Does TopNote have texting?
