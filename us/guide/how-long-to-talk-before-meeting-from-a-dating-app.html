How long should you talk to someone from a dating app before meeting?

There's no magic number. Talk until you feel comfortable, then hear their voice on a short call before you meet.

Why a long chat isn't always better

Weeks of texting can build a picture in your head. Real life may not match it.

Why a short chat isn't always better either

Meeting someone you know nothing about can feel risky. A little back and forth helps you check that they're a real person.

Add a voice call in the middle

A few minutes of talking tells you more than a week of messages. You hear their tone, their laugh, and how easy it feels. Here's why a call before a first date helps.

Signs you're ready to meet

  • The conversation feels easy and goes both ways.
  • Their story stays the same from one chat to the next.
  • You'd happily say yes to a coffee in a public place.

FAQ

Is it bad to meet someone quickly?
Not at all. If a short call felt good and you feel comfortable, a quick meetup in a public place is a fine next step.
Is texting for weeks a red flag?
Not always, but it can build a picture that real life doesn't match. If it keeps dragging on with no plan, it's okay to ask for a call.
What if they never want to talk on the phone?
That's worth noticing. Someone who is happy to talk will usually say yes to a short call.
