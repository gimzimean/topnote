How do you date when you work long hours?

T.Beet EditorsNov 6, 2026 · 2 min read

30-SECOND SUMMARY

Set a few fixed slots each week and keep first meetings short. A quick voice call can tell you if someone is worth an evening.

01Block time like any other plan

Pick two evenings a week and protect them. If nothing is on the calendar, work wins every time.

Small and regular beats big and rare. Even 15 minutes counts.

02Check the vibe before you commit

A short call can save you a long, awkward dinner. You hear their voice and their humor in a few minutes.

If you want ideas for what to say, read our first call tips. You can also see why a call before a first date helps.

03Keep first meetings short and easy

Coffee or a quick walk is enough. You can always stay longer if it goes well.

04Be honest about your schedule

Say it early: "My weeks are packed, but I'm free Thursday." Good people appreciate the clarity.

Q&AFAQ

Is it okay to have a short first chat before meeting?

Yes. A few minutes on the phone is a low-cost way to see if you want to spend an evening together.

How often should I date if I'm busy?

Pick a rhythm you can keep up, like a couple of evenings a week. Regular and small is easier to sustain than occasional bursts.
