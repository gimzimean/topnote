How do you know if a first date went well?

A good first date feels easy and a little too short. You both talked, you both listened, and you'd like to see them again.

Signs it went well

  • The conversation got easier as it went on.
  • You laughed, even once.
  • They asked about you and remembered what you said.
  • Time moved fast and neither of you rushed to leave.
  • You left in a better mood than you arrived.

Signs to read gently

A fast text afterward is nice, but it's just one clue. Some people are slow texters and still very interested.

Awkward moments don't mean it went badly. Most first meetings have a few.

Check in with yourself first

Ask one simple question: did I feel relaxed around them? Your answer matters as much as theirs.

If you're unsure, give it a day before you decide.
