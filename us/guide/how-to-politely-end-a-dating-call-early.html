How do you politely end a phone call with a dating app match?

T.Beet EditorsNov 19, 2026 · 2 min read

30-SECOND SUMMARY

Say one kind, clear sentence, like "I'm going to head out, thanks for talking." Then say goodbye and end the call.

01Why a short goodbye works

You don't owe anyone a long call you're not enjoying. A warm, simple exit is kinder than going silent.

02Lines you can use

  • "I'm going to head out. Thanks for talking."
  • "This was nice, but I don't think we're a match. Take care."
  • "I have to go now. I hope you have a good night."

03If it's just awkward

Give it one more easy question first, like "What was the best part of your week?" If it still feels off, use your line.

04If you're not interested at all

Be honest without being harsh. Use "I" words, like "I'm not feeling a connection," and skip the long explanation.

Q&AFAQ

Is it rude to hang up early on a date call?

Not if you're kind about it. A short thank-you and a clear goodbye is better than going quiet or making an excuse.

What if they keep talking and won't let me go?

Pick a calm moment and say your line again, a little firmer. "I need to go now, take care." Then end the call.

Does Beet make me stay on the full call?

No. The 1:1 call ends on its own at 3 minutes, and you can hang up, report or block anytime. If you don't say yes to photos, they aren't told.
