How do you avoid catfishing on dating apps?

The simplest defense is to hear the person early. A real voice in the first few minutes, plus apps that check faces, makes fake profiles much harder to pull off.

Red flags

  • They avoid calls or video for weeks.
  • Their story changes when you ask simple follow-ups.
  • They ask for money, gift cards, or crypto. Report this right away.
  • Every photo looks like a professional shoot.

What helps

  • Talk early. A short call is hard to fake.
  • Face checks. On TopNote every member does a quick face check that's compared with their profile photos.
  • Human review. Our team reviews every member before their first call.
  • Report and block. Available on every screen with another person.
