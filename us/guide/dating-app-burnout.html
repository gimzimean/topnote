What should you do when dating apps leave you burned out?

Give yourself a real break, then come back with limits: set times, a small daily effort, and a clear idea of what you want. Burnout usually means the routine is too heavy, not that something is wrong with you.

Notice the signs

You open the app out of habit, not hope. Messages feel like a chore.

That's your cue to change how you date, not to try harder.

Take a break, or just set limits

A full break can reset things. If that feels like too much, turn off notifications and pick one short window a day.

Close the app when the window ends.

Get clear on what you want

Write down three things you actually want in a partner. It makes it easier to skip chats that go nowhere.

Fewer, better conversations beat endless scrolling.

Try a lower-effort way to meet people

Talking is often easier than texting back and forth. A short voice call shows warmth fast, without hours of small talk.

There is no magic number. Many people try a week or two and see how they feel, then come back with limits.
Do I have to delete the apps to recover?
No. Turning off notifications and setting a daily time window can help just as much.
