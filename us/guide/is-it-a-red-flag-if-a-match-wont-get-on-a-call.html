Is it a red flag if a match won't get on a call?

Not always. One no is a yellow flag, but a pattern of dodging calls while pushing you to another app is worth a closer look.

Why people say no to calls

Lots of people feel awkward on camera or on the phone. That alone doesn't mean anything is wrong.

Some are busy, some are shy, and some just text better than they talk.

When it starts to look like a pattern

Watch for someone who always has a reason not to talk. It matters more if they also cancel plans or won't meet in a public place.

Another sign is pushing you to leave the app right away. Take that seriously, and read our guides on avoiding catfishing and talking on the phone before a first date.

A low-pressure way to ask

Offer something small, like a quick voice-only call. No camera makes it easier to say yes.

Try: "Want to do a 5-minute call this week? Totally fine if not."

How TopNote handles it

On TopNote, every match starts with a 3-minute voice call, so nobody has to negotiate it. Photos open only if both say yes after the call.

Calls run every evening, 5-11 PM Eastern Time. They are never recorded, and you can hang up, report or block anytime.

FAQ

What can I suggest instead of a video call?
Try a short voice-only call. It is lower pressure, and many people find it easier than being on camera.
Should I give out my phone number to get them on a call?
You don't have to. Use a call inside the app first, and share your number only when you feel comfortable.
What if they keep making excuses?
You can stop the conversation and move on. You don't owe anyone endless patience, and you can report or block anytime.
