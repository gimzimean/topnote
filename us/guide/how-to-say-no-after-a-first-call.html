How do you politely say no to a second date after a first call?

T.Beet EditorsNov 7, 2026 · 2 min read

30-SECOND SUMMARY

Be kind, brief and clear, and say it soon. A short "I enjoyed talking, but I don't feel a match" is enough.

01Keep it short

You don't owe a long reason. One or two warm sentences land better than a speech.

Try: "Thanks for the call, I really enjoyed it. I don't think we're a match, but I wish you the best."

02Don't leave them guessing

A vague "maybe later" feels nice for a moment and worse over time. A clear no lets the other person move on.

If you can't say it live, a short text works too.

03Skip the blame

Talk about your own feeling, not their flaws. "It didn't click for me" is true and kind.

04If they push back

Repeat it once, calmly. If they keep pushing, you can stop replying, block or report.

Not sure how to handle a call itself? See first call tips and why a call before a first date helps.

05How Beet handles it

After a 3-minute call, photos open only if both say yes. A one-sided yes is never revealed, so there's no awkward "no" to deliver.

Q&AFAQ

Do I have to explain why I'm not interested?

No. You don't owe a long reason, and a short honest line is kinder than a vague one.

Is it okay to just stop replying?

It's easier on both people to send one clear message. Silence leaves the other person guessing.

How does this work on Beet?

After a call, photos open only if both say yes. If you don't say yes, the other person isn't told.
