How do you politely say no to a second date after a first call?

Be kind, brief and clear, and say it soon. A short "I enjoyed talking, but I don't feel a match" is enough.

Keep it short

You don't owe a long reason. One or two warm sentences land better than a speech.

Try: "Thanks for the call, I really enjoyed it. I don't think we're a match, but I wish you the best."

Don't leave them guessing

A vague "maybe later" feels nice for a moment and worse over time. A clear no lets the other person move on.

If you can't say it live, a short text works too.

Skip the blame

Talk about your own feeling, not their flaws. "It didn't click for me" is true and kind.

If they push back

Repeat it once, calmly. If they keep pushing, you can stop replying, block or report.
