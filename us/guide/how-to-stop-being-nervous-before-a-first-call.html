How do you stop being nervous before a first call with someone new?

Slow your breathing, pick one easy question, and focus on listening instead of performing. Nerves before a first call are normal, and the other person is usually just as nervous.

Give your body a minute first

Take five slow breaths before you dial. Breathe in for four counts and out for six.

Shake out your hands and sit somewhere quiet. A calm room helps a calm voice.

Plan one opener, not a script

Pick one easy question you can ask right away, like what they did last weekend. That's your way in, and everything after can be a follow-up.

Need ideas? See 10 easy first-call openers.

Listen more than you talk

Ask about their answer instead of planning your next line. Listening takes the pressure off, and people enjoy being heard.

Remember what the call is for

It's just a short chat to see if you'd like to talk more. It doesn't have to go perfectly, and it's fine if it isn't a match.

FAQ

Is it normal to feel nervous before a first call?
Yes. Meeting anyone new makes most people a little nervous, and the other person is likely feeling it too.
What if I freeze up during the call?
Pause, take a breath, and ask a follow-up about something they just said. A short silence feels much longer to you than to them.
Does TopNote make first calls less stressful?
Calls are voice only for 3 minutes, with no photos, so you can skip worrying about how you look. If you don't click, you can let it end, and a one-sided yes to photos is never revealed.
