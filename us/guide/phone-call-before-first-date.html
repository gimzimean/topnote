Should you talk on the phone before a first date?

Yes, a short call can help, as long as it's low pressure and you both want it. Keep it to a few minutes so it feels like a hello, not an interview.

Why a quick call helps

A voice tells you things a profile can't, like warmth, humor and how easy it is to talk to someone.

It can also make the first meetup less awkward, because you've already heard each other laugh.

How to ask without pressure

Try something simple like, "Want to do a quick call this week before we meet?" Make it easy to say no.

If they'd rather skip it, take them at their word and move on to a plan.

What to talk about

Keep it light. Ask about their week, something they're into lately, or a place they love.

Skip the heavy topics for later. For ideas, see our first call tips.

When to skip the call

If long calls are starting to feel like a replacement for meeting, it may be time to make a plan. A short call and a coffee beats hours of phone time.

FAQ

How long should a call before a first date be?
Short is fine. Ten to fifteen minutes is plenty, and even 3 minutes can tell you a lot.
What if I hate talking on the phone?
That's okay, and you can say so kindly. Offer a quick voice note or go straight to a casual meetup in a public place instead.
Is it a red flag if someone won't get on a call?
Not always. Some people are just shy about it, but a person who dodges every plan to talk or meet is worth a second look.
