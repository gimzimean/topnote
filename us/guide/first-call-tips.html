What should you talk about on a first call?

Start with one narrow, easy question, then ask about their answer. That's enough to fill 3 minutes and see if you click.

10 openers that work

  1. What did you do last weekend?
  2. What's a place near you that you'd take a friend to?
  3. What are you into lately?
  4. Best thing you ate this week?
  5. Morning person or night person?
  6. What's something you're looking forward to?
  7. What's your go-to way to unwind?
  8. Last show you couldn't stop watching?
  9. What would your friends say you're great at?
  10. What's a small thing that made your day recently?

Three quick rules

  • Ask about their answer, not the next question on your list.
  • Keep it light. Save the heavy stuff for later.
  • Find a quiet spot. Good audio helps more than you'd think.

FAQ

What if the call goes quiet?
Ask a follow-up about the last thing they said. "Wait, how did that happen?" works almost every time.
Can we keep talking after 3 minutes?
Yes. If you both tap for more, you get 3 more minutes.
What if I don't click with them?
Just let the call end. If you don't say yes to photos, they aren't told.
