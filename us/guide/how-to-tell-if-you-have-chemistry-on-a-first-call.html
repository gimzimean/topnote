How do you know if you have chemistry on a first call?

T.Beet EditorsNov 15, 2026 · 2 min read

30-SECOND SUMMARY

Chemistry on a first call usually feels like easy back-and-forth and wanting to hear what they say next. It's a feeling in the moment, not a test you can pass or fail.

01Signs it's going well

  • You lose track of time a little.
  • You laugh without trying.
  • You both ask questions, not just answer them.
  • You're a bit sad when the call ends.

02What not to read into

A quiet moment isn't a bad sign. Nerves can make anyone sound flat in the first minute.

A great voice isn't chemistry either. Notice how the talk feels, not how it sounds.

03Check in with yourself after

Ask one question: do I want to hear more from this person? If the answer is yes, that's your signal.

If you're unsure, that's useful too. Give it a day, then go with your gut.

04Why a short call helps

On Beet the call is 3 minutes, voice only, so you feel the conversation before photos get a vote. After the call, photos open only if you both say yes.

Q&AFAQ

What if I feel nothing on the first call?

That's okay. Nerves can hide a spark, but you also don't have to force one. Trust how you feel and move on if it's a no.

Can chemistry grow after a first call?

Sometimes a little warmth shows up with more time. Still, a clear "no" is a fine answer.

What if only one of us feels it?

On Beet, a one-sided yes is never revealed. Photos open only if you both say yes.
