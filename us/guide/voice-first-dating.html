What is voice-first dating?

Voice-first dating means you talk before you see photos. A 3-minute call usually tells you whether you click, before you spend a whole evening finding out.

Why voice first?

On most apps you match on photos, text for days, then meet. Only then do you learn if the conversation works. If it doesn't, the evening is gone.

Talking first flips that. If it doesn't click, you lose 3 minutes, not a night.

How TopNote does it

  1. Talk for 3 minutes. No photos, no numbers. Just a voice call in the app.
  2. Photos. They show up only if you both say yes.
  3. Swap numbers. After photos, if you both want to. That's a match.

If only one of you says yes, the other person never finds out.

FAQ

Is voice-first dating awkward?
Less than you'd think. 3 minutes is short, and one easy question like "What did you do last weekend?" is enough to start.
Do I ever see photos?
Yes. After the call, photos open if you both say yes.
Is the call recorded?
No. TopNote never records calls.
